# Splinterhall credits

Made by Eric with Claude. Every asset is public domain (CC0) or under a licence that only asks for credit; nothing share-alike and nothing non-commercial. Each asset folder has its own detailed CREDITS.md (`assets/audio/music/`, `assets/audio/sfx/`, `assets/hdri/`, `assets/fonts/`).

## Music tracks

The default music (Options → Music → Source: Tracks) plays six songs pre-rendered from the game's own band (the live generator, made from the samples below) and these Creative Commons tracks. Details per file in `assets/audio/tracks/CREDITS.md`.

- **"Raid_FolkMetal"** by PeriTune (https://peritune.com/blog/2019/08/26/raid_folkmetal/), licensed under CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Re-encoded to 96 kbps MP3 and loudness-matched.
- **"Raid_FolkMetal2"** by PeriTune (https://peritune.com/blog/2020/01/17/raid_folkmetal2/), licensed under CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Re-encoded to 96 kbps MP3 and loudness-matched.

## Music samples

The live band (Source: Live generator) is synthesised from recorded single notes and hits, and so are the pre-rendered Splinterhall band songs.

- **FluidR3 GM** by Frank Wen, rendered by Benjamin Gleitzman's MIDI.js Soundfonts (https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts), used under CC BY 3.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). Electric guitar, electric bass and bagpipe notes, trimmed, retuned, looped and re-encoded.
- VS Chamber Orchestra: Community Edition (Versilian Studios, CC0): strings, solo violin, brass, orchestral percussion
- Versilian Community Sample Library (Versilian Studios, CC0): snare, hi-hats, ride, crashes, war drum, frame drum, timpani, recorder
- Sonic Pi sample pack (freesound.org recordings by menegass and Zajo, CC0): kick, toms, crash, snare crack
