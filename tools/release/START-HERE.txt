SPLINTERHALL · Prototype 4

FILES
  splinterhall-itch.zip     Upload to itch.io as an HTML game (index.html is at the zip root).
  splinterhall-cover.png    The itch.io cover image, 630 x 500.
  splinterhall-source.zip   The whole repository (no node_modules, no dist), ready for GitHub.
  splinterhall-wifi-host.zip  Optional extra itch.io download (kind: "Other", not the HTML game): the game
                            plus a small Python server for brawls on one Wi-Fi with no internet, hosted
                            from a Steam Deck, Linux PC or Mac. Not needed for the in-game QR pairing.

ITCH.IO SETTINGS
  Kind of project: HTML
  Embed options: "Click to launch in fullscreen" off; viewport 1280 x 720; tick "Mobile friendly"
  (orientation: Landscape) and "Fullscreen button".
  SharedArrayBuffer support is not needed.

BUILDING FROM SOURCE
  npm install
  npm run build             writes dist/web/ and the zips
  node tools/cover.mjs dist/splinterhall-cover.png

SUGGESTED ITCH.IO DESCRIPTION
  A physics stick brawl for up to 8 fighters, on your phone, tablet, Steam Deck or desktop.
  One stick, two buttons, and a world made of hundreds of physics blocks: carve it with rockets,
  smash it with a war hammer, rip it out with a tractor beam, swing through it on a web line,
  open portals in it, or blow a horn and let a stone serpent chew through it.
  Every level comes from an 8-character code, and the editor turns your changes into a code to share.
  Fighters are 3D skeletons with a whole repertoire: jabs, crosses and hooks, haymakers, uppercuts,
  roundhouses, leg sweeps, flying kicks and dropkicks; an axe that whirls round you, a sword that
  lunges, a hammer that spins. Instrumental viking, battle, power and folk metal plays throughout.
  Play against AI, or with friends on the internet or one Wi-Fi with no internet.

  Controls: on a phone, the left thumb runs, jumps (up) and crouches (down); the right thumb plays
  one square: swipe to hit where you swipe, up for an uppercut, down for a slam (a stomp dive in the
  air), sweep across for a haymaker, swipe along the bottom to kick (up: roundhouse, crouching: leg
  sweep, at a run: flying kick, long: dropkick), tap to throw what you hold. The eye shows the whole
  arena for a moment. Push into a wall in the air and push up to kick off it.
  Keys: arrows/WASD, Space jump, X hit (Up + X uppercut, Down + X slam), C kick, V haymaker,
  E throw, Tab view. Pad: A jump, X hit, B kick, Y haymaker, RB throw, Select view.
  Learn on the title screen teaches every move.
