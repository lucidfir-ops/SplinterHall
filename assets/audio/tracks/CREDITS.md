# Music tracks: credits

The track player (Options → Music → Source: Tracks, the default) plays the files listed in `tracks.json`.

## Splinterhall band (game original)

The six `gen-*.mp3` songs were rendered offline from the game's own live generator by `tools/music/pregen.mjs`.
They are made from the music samples credited in `../music/CREDITS.md`: VS Chamber Orchestra: Community Edition and
the Versilian Community Sample Library (CC0, Versilian Studios), the Sonic Pi sample pack (CC0), and FluidR3 GM by
Frank Wen, rendered by Benjamin Gleitzman's MIDI.js Soundfonts (CC BY 3.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).
