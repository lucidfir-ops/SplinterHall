# Music samples: credits and licences

Every file in this folder was made by `tools/samples/build_bank.py` from the recordings listed below: trimmed, faded, pitch-measured and resampled onto the semitone, loop-baked where sustained, level-matched and encoded as mono 44.1 kHz MP3. Nothing here is share-alike or non-commercial.
